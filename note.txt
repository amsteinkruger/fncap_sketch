2026/09/02

Using simple climate covariates for wildfire risk is unsatisfying.

Some reasonable components of perceived wildfire risk:

 - Exposure to estimated wildfire risk through messaging (NFDRS, CFFDRS).
 - Exposure to realized wildfire through promixity -- but with weighting over distance.
 - Exposure to realized wildfire through environmental similarity (again with weighting over distance). 

So, estimate NFDRS or an equivalent (CFFDRS) and improve on present 0-15-30 distance metrics. 

Could be worth a practices paper using wildfire risk mitigation as a response. 

And some equivalent to WLFP- and/or FSIM-style wildfire risk estimates would be nice for comparison.
